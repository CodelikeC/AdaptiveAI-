# Code of Conduct – Adaptive AI

Adaptive AI is a community for responsible, ethical, and innovative research.  
We expect all contributors to uphold a professional and inclusive environment.

### 💡 Our Principles
- Treat others with respect and openness.
- Avoid personal attacks or discriminatory language.
- Share knowledge responsibly.
- Remember that AI ethics begins with human behavior.

### 🧭 Enforcement
Violations can be reported via GitHub Issues or directly to maintainers.  
Serious breaches may result in suspension or permanent removal from the project.
